Tips on using Facer with your Apple Watch: - Install the Facer phone app on your iPhone - Make sure the Facer watch app is installed on your Apple Watch - Allow connecting to HealthKit to display Steps and Heart Rate data in faces - Pick a free face (or premium if subscribed) in the Facer phone app and tap the button to send it to your watch - Accept the prompt from your iPhone’s Apple Watch app to add this face to your watch - A placeholder preview for the face will appear in your iPhone’s Apple Watch app - Set the face to your watch from within the iPhone Apple Watch app (even before the preview fully displays) - After several seconds, the face will download to your watch and the preview will eventually appear in your iPhone Apple Watch app (which may require app re-launch) - Tap the face on your Apple Watch to get the Facer watch app to refresh data complications (if included in the face) more often than Apple’s system cadence (which is approx every 15min unless your watch is taxed and Apple OS further throttles refresh) FEEDBACK & TROUBLESHOOTING Have issues using our app or faces or are dissatisfied in any way?
